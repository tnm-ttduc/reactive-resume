## Deployment-specific policy

TNM HR Platform can be operated by different organizations. The organization that runs your deployment is responsible for
publishing the applicable privacy policy, identifying the data controller, and providing a privacy contact.

## Data handled by the platform

Depending on enabled features, a deployment may process:

- Account and authentication information
- Resume and job-application content
- Uploaded profile images and attachments
- Usage, security, and audit logs
- AI prompts and generated results when AI features are enabled

Contact your TNM HR Platform administrator for retention periods, subprocessors, data-location details, access or deletion
requests, and the legal basis that applies to your deployment.

<Warning>
  Operators must replace this deployment notice with a policy reviewed for their organization and jurisdiction before making
  the service available to end users.
</Warning>
